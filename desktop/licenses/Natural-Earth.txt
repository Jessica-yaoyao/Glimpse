Natural Earth geographic coverage data (public domain)
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_map_units.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The bundled JSON retains the CHN map-unit geometry for offline coordinate
adapter coverage, excluding the separate Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan units.
It is not displayed as a boundary map. Natural Earth's generalized geometry
is not a precision geofence; border/coastline accuracy remains a limitation.
