Natural overcast daylight, subdued film colors, fine tactile grain. Viewed slightly from above. Bag centered in lower middle, occupies x=18%-82%, y=32%-75%. The bag is accidentally shaped like a curled sleeping fox: two folded triangular handles at upper left around x30% y38%, a small pointed fold pointing left at x23% y54%, a rounded crumpled body at x55% y53%, a long folded end curling along bottom from x77% y62% toward x35% y70%. It must clearly be a real discarded PLASTIC BAG with wrinkles, creases, handles and plastic reflections, NOT an animal or sculpture. Lots of empty pavement above. No actual fox, no eyes, no drawn lines, no text, no watermark. Quiet creative photography. Return image and save local file.
